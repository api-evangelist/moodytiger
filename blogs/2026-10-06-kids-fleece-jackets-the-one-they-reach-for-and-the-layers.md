There is a specific morning, somewhere in late October, when the hoodie stops being enough. The kid walks to the door, feels the air, and comes back for something warmer — and whatever you hand them next is the jacket they'll live in until spring. This page is about choosing that jacket well, because the right one gets worn every day and the wrong one lives on a hook.
